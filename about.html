COMPANY GOVERNANCE & PROFILE

An Authoritative Partner in Civil Infrastructure & Public Works

DAT Construction is an Indore-based infrastructure contractor specializing in civil construction, environmental maintenance, water processing systems, and institutional grounds development.

With documented tender participation and recorded contract wins spanning premiere institutional hubs—including Raja Ramanna Centre for Advanced Technology (RRCAT)—DAT Construction has established a reputation for rigorous site execution, quality management, and strict safety compliance.

Operating directly out of our Rajendra Nagar headquarters in Indore, our management team oversees multi-disciplinary engineering crews trained to fulfill complex BOQs with audit-ready transparency.
